# From the Block to Blockchain

**FTBTB** is the canonical research, narrative, and provenance repository for *From the Block to Blockchain* by Major Dream Williams.

This repository is not merely a manuscript folder. It is the living source-of-truth layer for the book, its historical research, interviews, artifacts, media, questions, claims, and evolving intellectual framework.

## Core thesis

Major Dream Williams's life is the lens through which the book examines a larger question:

> Who becomes the steward of civilization's memory in the age of artificial intelligence?

The book follows a journey from the Bronx through athletics, finance, cryptocurrency, blockchain, artificial intelligence, entrepreneurship, travel, and global systems. Beneath that journey is a deeper inquiry into ownership, memory, provenance, power, narrative, wisdom, and stewardship.

## Repository principles

1. **Preserve the voice.** Raw dictation and lived experience remain distinct from edited prose.
2. **Separate evidence from interpretation.** Documented facts, contested claims, personal observations, and open questions must be labeled clearly.
3. **Maintain provenance.** Every significant claim should connect to a source, interview, artifact, or clearly marked personal account.
4. **Version the thinking.** Earlier drafts are part of the intellectual record, not disposable mistakes.
5. **Invite inquiry rather than coercion.** The work should provoke thought without pretending that every question is settled.
6. **Protect people and sensitive information.** Private, legally sensitive, or identifying material must not be published without review.

## Claim classifications

Every material claim should be marked using one of these labels:

- `DOCUMENTED` — supported by credible primary or high-quality secondary sources.
- `CONTESTED` — meaningful disagreement exists among credible sources or interpretations.
- `PERSONAL ACCOUNT` — based on Major's lived experience, memory, or direct observation.
- `INFERENCE` — a reasoned conclusion drawn from stated evidence.
- `OPEN QUESTION` — unresolved and presented for investigation.
- `CREATIVE / METAPHORICAL` — used for narrative or philosophical expression rather than factual assertion.
